Data info

NASA FIRMS NOAA-20 VIIRS thermal detections, filtered to Indonesia. Fetching downloads the latest available rolling 24-hour feed. The actual observation dates are shown on the page.

Hotspots can include fires and other heat sources. They are not fire boundaries, burned area, or a count of distinct wildfires. Forest and land drawings represent the performance model's local patches; their size and trees are schematic, not measured vegetation or burned extent. Multiple detections in one patch share its drawing; original observations remain available under Next point.

Country filtering uses Natural Earth's generalized boundary and may omit very small islands or coastal detections.

Rain, wetness, moisture, and residual heat are a separate performance simulation. The slower heat response is peat-inspired; it does not classify these hotspots as peat fires or predict real fire behaviour. NASA observations stay unchanged.

Each participant supplies one fixed water budget per second while holding. Extra fingers do not add water. Sweeping spreads that budget; overlapping holds add water. Patches and the brush use geographic coordinates, so water stays in place when the map moves. Timing controls adjust the surface response, return of flames, ember cooling, and out confirmation. Extinction requires continued rain, high moisture and low residual heat for 4 seconds.

The cloud and rain scale with the geographic brush area. Rain falls freely beneath the cloud; water is applied to the circular area under it. Each forest patch responds to its own wetness and heat. Embers and smoke can remain after flames disappear; out patches retain charred trunks and ash.

The brush readout averages wetness, moisture, and heat over its circular footprint. Partly covered patches contribute in proportion to their covered area; untouched patches contribute zero. Point inspection still shows the selected patch separately.

Moisture tuning: dM/dt = r/1.2 − M/180, with M clamped to 0–1. These are rehearsal parameters, not measured physics. Unfinished patches regain heat when local rain stops; retained moisture does not continue cooling them unattended. Fully out patches stay out. One shared simulation runs on the laptop server, including when this display is hidden. Wetness and heat save separately from the NASA snapshot. Phones send normalized positions within their assigned region and never overwrite shared state. Rain stops on release or connection loss; stale holds expire after two seconds. Reset simulation restores dry, hot patches without changing the source snapshot.
